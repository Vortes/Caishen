# Separate loan forecasts from confirmed activity

Use confirmed lender records for actual loan balances and interest charges, with connections or uploaded statements providing evidence for automated reconciliation. Keep calculated interest and payoff forecasts separate so approximation differences do not become invented charges or balance adjustments. Variable-rate forecasts assume the latest confirmed rate continues, disclose that assumption, retain rate changes with their effective dates, and recalculate when new terms take effect.

Pair loans with payment categories showing the required payment, funded amount, and estimated payoff date. Extra-payment previews illustrate possible outcomes without reallocating money or initiating a bank payment; funding an extra payment remains the user's decision.
