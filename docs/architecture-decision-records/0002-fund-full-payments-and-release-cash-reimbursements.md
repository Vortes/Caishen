# Fund full payments and return cash reimbursements to Ready to Assign

Envelopes allocate actual liquid cash: a shared category funds the full amount its owner pays or owes, with no separate reimbursement buffer and no assignable money created from expected repayments. A received cash reimbursement reduces the outstanding amount owed and returns to Ready to Assign rather than refilling the original category, while remaining distinct from new earnings in reports. This keeps allocation under the user's control and requires budget cash availability to remain separate from personal-share spending reports; it does not turn an expected reimbursement or a card statement credit into a cash deposit.

Funding plans are entirely user-controlled: the agent neither proposes plans nor allocates Ready to Assign cash to categories. This is separate from the already-authorized bookkeeping movements, such as card-payment reserves and released reimbursements, and from explicit rules for covering overspending between categories.

Use [YNAB's target types](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/getting-started-with-targets-ryAEP08xC) and [documented target behavior](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/how-to-use-targets-rk5kkI9ks) as the reference rather than inventing a reduced replacement: weekly, monthly, yearly, and custom schedules; contribution, refill, and balance goals; repetition and current-month snoozing where supported; and paired card/loan targets. Targets display funding needs and progress while assignments remain user-controlled. In a shared category, the funding goal concerns the full cash its owner plans to pay or owes, independently of the personal share shown in spending reports.

When a net settlement is recorded, the actual payment clears the net amount owed and the offset clears the corresponding mutual obligations; cash reserved for those offset obligations returns to Ready to Assign without recording an invented deposit. Funded credit-card purchases move their allocated cash into the card-payment reserve, and actual card payments consume that reserve rather than counting as new spending or releasing cash for reassignment.

A posted statement credit can release existing card-payment reserves into Ready to Assign only to the extent they exceed the cash needed for the remaining card balance. An expected credit does not release funds, and a posted credit does not create a bank deposit.

Retain [YNAB's monthly rollover behavior](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/when-the-month-rolls-over-a-guide-rkyyd6qC9): positive available balances carry forward, uncovered cash overspending reduces the following month's Ready to Assign, and uncovered credit spending remains debt requiring funding. Reimbursement claims and shared obligations remain visible across months without changing those cash-availability rules.
